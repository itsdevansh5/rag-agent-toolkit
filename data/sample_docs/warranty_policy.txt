Warranty Policy

All products come with a 1-year limited warranty covering manufacturing
defects. The warranty does not cover damage from misuse, accidental
drops, water exposure, or unauthorized repairs.

Gadget Max includes an extended 2-year warranty due to its premium
component tier. Widget Lite, as our budget product line, carries a
90-day warranty instead of the standard 1-year term.

To file a warranty claim, customers should contact support with proof of
purchase and a description of the defect. Approved claims result in a
free repair or replacement at our discretion; we do not offer cash refunds
for warranty claims outside the standard 30-day return window.
