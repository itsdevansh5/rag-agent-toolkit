Shipping Policy

Standard shipping takes 5-7 business days and is free on orders over $50.
Orders under $50 have a flat shipping fee of $6.99. Expedited shipping
(2-3 business days) is available at checkout for an additional $12.

We currently ship only within the continental United States. Orders are
processed within 1 business day of purchase, excluding weekends and public
holidays. Once an order ships, a tracking number is emailed automatically.

Widget Pro and Widget Lite ship from our primary warehouse and are
typically fastest to arrive. Gadget Max ships from a secondary facility due
to limited stock and may take an additional 1-2 days to dispatch.
